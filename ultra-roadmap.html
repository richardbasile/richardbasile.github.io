2027–2029 · Half Marathon to 100-Miler

Ultra Roadmap

Overview

2027 is a conservative base year built around one A-race — the Run to Feed the Hungry Half Marathon on Nov 13, with a goal of a PR rather than a fixed time. Five supporting races along the way serve as checkpoints for fitness and pacing, not goal races in their own right. From there, 2028 adds two 50Ks, and 2029 is a conditional 50-miler and 100-miler at Canal Corridor — contingent on how recovery goes at each step.

2027–2029 Roadmap

2027 — Conservative base year, six races

  • Feb 21 — Timbuk Winter Challenge 22K (trail), controlled effort
  • Apr 3 — Franklinton 10 Miler, controlled effort
  • Jul 11 — Run Wild 5K, hard effort
  • Sep 11 — Sunbury 10K, controlled tempo
  • Sep 26 — Big Bad Wolfe 10 Miler, real fitness test
  • Nov 13 — Run to Feed the Hungry Half Marathon, A-race, PR attempt

Training focus: build my distance base first, then shift into strength, power, and speed work once that base is solid.

2028 — Two 50Ks

  • May — first 50K (Thunderbunny or Tie Dye)
  • Later in the year — second 50K as a checkpoint, reading how I recover and handle the distance a second time before committing further

2029 — Conditional 50-miler and 100-miler, contingent on how 2028 goes

  • Spring — first 50-miler
  • July — turn 60
  • October — Canal Corridor 100-miler, on a flat, fast course in the town where I grew up, contingent on clean recovery from the spring 50-miler, fueling/pacing holding up, and a clear doctor/PT check-in

Longer-term hope: a 13.1-mile long run every month, whether as a race or a solo training run, with effort level as the variable that flexes — not the distance.
