Three phases · Monday, Wednesday, Friday

Strength Plan

Overview

Three sessions a week in every phase, on the days between runs. The plan builds toward one main barbell lift for each area (squat, hinge, bench, row, with the dumbbell overhead press for shoulders) and a complement of the same pattern on another day. The glute and core routine (physical therapy) runs three days a week in every phase.

Phase Focus Target length Move on when
1 · Foundation Core, bodyweight, bands, dumbbells. 3 sets of everything. 4–6 weeks Three sessions a week for 3 straight weeks; top of the rep range on all 3 sets with clean form; split squats no longer leave you sore for Thursday
2 · Barbell intro Learn the barbell lifts at light weights; dumbbell lifts become complements. 4–6 weeks Every rep of every barbell set looks the same, and you’ve added weight for at least 2 weeks without form breaking down
3 · Main lifts One main lift per area plus a complement on another day; steady, small weight increases. Ongoing Switches between build, hold, maintenance and light with the 2027 season (below)

Started Oct 26, Phase 1 would end around the start of the winter base on Nov 30, which is when the season outline starts adding weight. Advance on the check, not the calendar.

Phase 1 · Foundation

Dumbbells, bands and bodyweight only, 3 sets of every exercise, plus the glute and core routine three days a week. Each future barbell lift has a dumbbell stand-in here (goblet squat, DB Romanian deadlift, DB bench, DB row), so the patterns are learned before the bar shows up.

Exercise Sets × Reps Pattern

  • Effort: 2–3 reps in reserve on every set. When all 3 sets reach the top of the range, add a little weight (or, for bodyweight moves, hold light dumbbells).
  • Pattern split: squat day gets the single-leg hinge (single-leg RDL); hinge day gets the split squat and front lunge, so no main pattern is paired with its own single-leg version.
  • Split squats and lunges: start at bodyweight. Split squats are known for heavy soreness at first; if Thursday’s run suffers, drop a set of front lunges first, then a set of split squats.
  • Friday legs stay light: step-ups and reverse lunges cause less soreness than front lunges or split squats, which protects Saturday.

Phase 3 in the 2027 Season

Phase 3 starts around early January and changes mode with the running season. The exercises stay the same; only sets and weight change.

Mode What it means

Maintenance Same weights, 2 working sets of the main lifts. Keep the single-leg work, calf raises and curls as written.
Light 1–2 sets of the main lifts at about 70% of working weight. Single-leg work at bodyweight; calf raises as written.

Guidelines (All Phases)
  • Adding weight: only when every rep of every set looks the same. Use small jumps. For bands, add reps first, then step back farther or move to a heavier band.
  • Barbell warm-up (Phases 2–3): empty bar 1 × 8–10, about 50% 1 × 5, about 75% 1 × 3, then working sets.
  • Rest: 2–3 minutes between main-lift sets, 60–90 seconds for accessories. Run the band superset back to back, resting after each round.
  • Lateral raises: light and strict. Your shoulders already work hard on pressing.
  • Calf raises: Monday and Friday in every phase. Slow, with a pause at the top and a 2–3 second lowering. Start at bodyweight; add a dumbbell or go single-leg once 15 reps is easy.
  • Weekly volume: about 6 working sets a week each for quads, hamstrings and chest (Phase 1 runs higher on quads because everything is 3 sets at bodyweight). Biceps get 6 direct sets: curls on Monday and Wednesday. Glutes are covered by the leg work and the PT routine; shoulders and triceps by the pressing.
  • Carry before curls: curls come last on Wednesday so your grip is fresh for the farmer’s carry.
  • If Saturday feels flat: drop a set of Friday’s lunges first (reverse lunges or step-ups in Phase 1, reverse lunges in Phases 2–3), then a set of single-leg RDLs, then skip Friday’s calf raises (Monday’s still count).
  • Race weeks: Wednesday is upper body plus calf raises, and Friday is upper body only. Pick up the normal plan at the next session once your legs feel recovered.
  • Down weeks: when running has a down week, lifting drops to 2 sets at the same weights.
  • Winter base: keep Friday’s single-leg work at bodyweight or light dumbbells while long runs climb toward 90–100 minutes.
  • If Thursday feels flat: drop a set of Wednesday’s lunge or split squat work.
Glute & Core Routine (All Phases)

Part of physical therapy. Do it three days a week in every phase: 2–3 circuits, after a session or on its own.

  • Glute bridge — 10–15
  • Dead bug — 8–10/side
  • Lateral leg raise — 12/side
  • Clamshells — 12–15/side
  • Donkey kicks — 12–15/side
  • Push-ups — 10–12

2027 Season Outline

A proposed shape for the year. The Cap City Half and the Run to Feed the Hungry Half are the goal races. Timbuk is a fun trail run, and the others are checkpoints. The winter builds the base, the spring builds to Cap City, speed and strength take the early summer, and the late summer and fall build to Run to Feed the Hungry.

  • Same rules all year: a down week every 3–4 weeks, one hard session a week with an easy day after it, the two-run minimum in busy weeks, and a recovery week after each goal race.
