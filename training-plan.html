14-Week Hybrid Strength + Half Marathon Build

Training Plan

Overview

Weekly mileage and lifting by day, building toward the Johnstown Turkey Trot Half Marathon on Nov 21. Focus is aerobic base, durability, and holding onto strength, with tempo effort reintroduced on Wednesdays. Monday pairs a morning strength session with an evening easy run; Tuesday is an easy 2-mile run or 30 min cross-training, your choice; Wednesday pairs strength with the week's tempo-effort run; Thursday is an easy run with strides; Friday is upper-body strength only plus optional easy cross-training, no run, to protect Saturday's long run; Saturday carries the long run, with periodic deload weeks along the way; Sunday is rest, except the Big Bad Wolfe 5K on Sep 27.

Easy Running, Tempo & Strides

Wednesday carries this cycle's quality work — a tempo effort at 150–158 bpm, sized to that week's mileage — but only in weeks where total volume is 15 miles or more. Weeks 1–5 stay easy on Wednesday even though they're Build phase; the base isn't there yet to absorb quality work. Tempo starts in Week 7 with a short segment bookended by a 1-mile warm-up and 1-mile cool-down, and grows to 3–4 sustained tempo miles by Weeks 8–12. Deload weeks (3, 6, 10), taper (13), and race week (14) drop Wednesday back to easy — don't chase the tempo prescription on those weeks.

Thursday's easy run includes 4–6 × 15–20 second strides near the end, full recovery walk between each — turnover and running economy with almost no fatigue cost.

Strength Program

Three sessions per week: Monday (full-body, heavy, barbell — back squat, deadlift, alternating press, row), Wednesday (lower-body, moderate, dumbbell/band), and Friday (upper-body only, moderate, dumbbell/band — no leg loading, so soreness doesn't land on Saturday's long run). Exercises and volume shift by phase and deload week. Click any Monday, Wednesday, or Friday cell in the grid for that week's exact sets, reps, and notes.

Core Routine

Same lower-back/hip stability routine as a separate session every strength day (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), one fixed target for the whole cycle — it doesn't scale down on deload or taper weeks the way the main lifts do.
