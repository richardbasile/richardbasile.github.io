Overview

Weekly training by day, prescribed in minutes. Running days are Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday; strength days are Tuesday and Friday; Sunday is rest. Running time builds from 145 to 195 minutes, rising no more than about 10% a week. Easy and long runs can use your run/walk method (), and time includes walk breaks. Strength Plan

Your Monday/Wednesday/Friday split condensed to two days around the running. Tuesday is the lower-body day, alternating squat weeks and deadlift weeks so your legs take only one heavy lift a week. It lands 24 hours before Wednesday’s quality run, so keep 2–3 reps in reserve on every working set and never grind a rep. Bulgarian split squats are out for this cycle because single-leg work leaves the most soreness. Friday stays upper body only, with no leg loading before Saturday’s long run. Overhead press moves to Tuesday so pressing is split across both days.
