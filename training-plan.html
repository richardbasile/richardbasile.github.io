12-Week Hybrid Strength + Half Marathon Build

Training Plan

Overview

Weekly training by day, prescribed in minutes, building toward the Johnstown Turkey Trot Half Marathon on Nov 21. Every run is easy effort using a run/walk method — no tempo work, no speed work, no strides. Monday is strength only (full-body) plus optional easy cross-training, no run; Tuesday carries the week's easy run; Wednesday is strength only (lower-body), no run; Thursday carries the week's other easy run; Friday is upper-body strength only, no run, to protect Saturday's long run; Saturday carries the long run, with periodic deload weeks along the way; Sunday is rest, except the Big Bad Wolfe 5K on Sep 27.

Run/Walk Method

Every run in training — Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday's long run — uses a run/walk approach at easy effort: currently . There's no tempo work and no strides anywhere in this cycle. Both races are the exception: the Big Bad Wolfe 5K (Week 4) and the Johnstown Turkey Trot (Week 12) are each raced at a genuine effort, faster than training pace — the 5K as a mid-cycle fitness check, the Turkey Trot as the actual goal race. The run/walk mechanics stay in play on both, just at a shorter walk break or faster run interval than the easy training ratio.

Strength Program

Three sessions per week: Monday (full-body, heavy, barbell — back squat, deadlift, alternating press, row), Wednesday (lower-body, moderate, dumbbell/band), and Friday (upper-body only, moderate, dumbbell/band — no leg loading, so soreness doesn't land on Saturday's long run). Exercises and volume shift by phase and deload week. Click any Monday, Wednesday, or Friday cell in the grid for that week's exact sets, reps, and notes.

Core Routine

Same lower-back/hip stability routine as a separate session every strength day (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), one fixed target for the whole cycle — it doesn't scale down on deload or taper weeks the way the main lifts do.
