12-Week Hybrid Strength + Half Marathon Build

Training Plan

Overview

Weekly training by day, prescribed in minutes, building toward the Johnstown Turkey Trot Half Marathon on Nov 21. Every run is easy effort using a run/walk method (). Strength days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday; running days are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday, with periodic deload weeks along the way. Sunday is rest, except the Big Bad Wolfe 5K on Sep 27.

Core Routine

Same lower-back/hip stability routine as a separate session every strength day (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), one fixed target for the whole cycle — it doesn't scale down on deload or taper weeks the way the main lifts do.
