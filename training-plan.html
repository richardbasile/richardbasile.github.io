Phase 1 · Monday, Wednesday, Friday

Strength Plan

Overview

Three full-body sessions a week, each built around one main lift: squat on Monday, deadlift on Wednesday, bench on Friday. Every main lift is done once a week and has a complement (a dumbbell, band or bodyweight version of the same pattern) on a different day. Phase 1 is about learning the movements and building the habit, so effort stays moderate and weight goes up only when the technique is solid.

The heavy leg work comes early in the week: squats on Monday, deadlifts and Bulgarian split squats on Wednesday. Friday is mostly upper body, with only lunges and calf raises for legs, so the weekend starts reasonably fresh.

Complements

Each main lift is paired with a lighter version of the same pattern on another day, so every movement gets practiced twice a week.

Main lift Complement
Back squat (Mon) Bulgarian split squat (Wed), lunges (Fri)
Bench press (Fri) DB bench press (Mon)
Barbell row (Fri) Banded row, banded straight-arm pulldown, band pull-aparts (Mon)
Deadlift (Wed) DB Romanian deadlift (Mon)
DB overhead press (Wed) Lateral raise (Fri)
Guidelines
  • Effort: keep 2–3 good reps in reserve on every working set of the main lifts (RPE 6–7).
  • Adding weight: only when every rep of every set looks the same. Use small jumps. For bands, add reps first, then step back farther or move to a heavier band.
  • Barbell warm-up: empty bar 1 × 8–10, about 50% 1 × 5, about 75% 1 × 3, then working sets.
  • Rest: 2–3 minutes between main-lift sets, 60–90 seconds for accessories.
  • Lateral raises: light and strict. Your shoulders already work hard on bench and overhead pressing.
  • Calf raises: slow, with a pause at the top and a 2–3 second lowering. Start at bodyweight; add a dumbbell or go single-leg once 15 reps is easy. If your calves feel heavy on Saturday, move them to Monday.
  • Carry before curls: curls come last on Wednesday so your grip is fresh for the farmer’s carry.
  • If Saturday feels flat: drop a set of Friday’s lunges first, then move calf raises to Monday.
  • Split squats and lunges: start at bodyweight or with light dumbbells. Bulgarian split squats are known for heavy soreness at first. If Thursday feels flat after Wednesday’s deadlifts and split squats, drop a split-squat set.
Glute & Core Routine

Separate from the main sessions. Do 2–3 circuits, after a session or on its own.

  • Pelvic tilt — 10–15
  • Glute bridge — 10–15
  • Dead bug — 8–10/side
  • Lateral leg raise — 12/side
  • Clamshells — 12–15/side
  • Donkey kicks — 12–15/side
  • Push-ups — 10–12

2027 Season Outline

A proposed shape for the year. The Cap City Half and the Run to Feed the Hungry Half are the goal races. Timbuk is a fun trail run, and the others are checkpoints. The winter builds the base, the spring builds to Cap City, speed and strength take the early summer, and the late summer and fall build to Run to Feed the Hungry.

  • Same rules all year: a down week every 3–4 weeks, one hard session a week with an easy day after it, the two-run minimum in busy weeks, and a recovery week after each goal race.
